How to Play

1

Join the Game

Enter the room code shown on the host's screen

2

Write Your Lie

See a trivia question with a blank — write a convincing fake answer

3

Find the Truth

Pick the real answer from all the lies (including other players' lies)

4

Score Points

Get points for finding the truth AND for fooling other players!
